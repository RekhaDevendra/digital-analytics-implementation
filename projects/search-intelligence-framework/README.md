# Search Intelligence Framework

## Integrating Market Demand, Organic Search Performance, and On-Site Search Behavior

## Business Context

Organizations often collect search data across multiple platforms, but the information remains fragmented across SEO, analytics, and market intelligence tools.

This project explores how search data can be unified into a single framework to better understand customer intent throughout the digital journey.

The objective was to connect:

* Market search demand
* Organic search visibility
* On-site search behavior

to provide actionable insights for content strategy, SEO prioritization, and digital experience improvements.

---

## Problem Statement

A stakeholder requested visibility into:

* Top on-site search queries by locale
* External Google search queries
* Search trends over a six-month period

The challenge was that each question required data from different platforms and represented different stages of the customer journey.

Rather than reporting metrics independently, the goal was to create a framework that connected customer behavior across the search ecosystem.

---

## Analytical Approach

### Stage 1: Market Demand Analysis

Objective:

Understand what topics, products, and questions customers are researching across the market.

Data Sources:

* Similarweb

* Search intelligence platforms
* Keyword trend datasets
* Search demand reports
* Trending Keywords
* Questions Queries


Key Questions:

* What topics are gaining demand?
* What questions are customers asking?
* Which product categories generate the most interest?

---

### Stage 2: External Search Analysis

Objective:

Understand how customers discover digital properties through search engines.

Data Sources:

* Google Search Console

Key Questions:

* Which queries drive visibility?
* Which topics generate organic traffic?
* How is search performance evolving over time?

---

### Stage 3: On-Site Search Analysis

Objective:

Understand customer intent after arriving on the website.

Data Sources:

* Google Analytics 4

Key Questions:

* What information are users seeking?
* Which topics require additional content support?
* What patterns exist across regions and languages?

## Key Learnings

### Search Data Tells a Customer Journey Story

Market demand data explains what customers are interested in.

Search engine data explains how customers discover content.

On-site search data explains what customers still need after arriving.

Combining all three creates a significantly stronger understanding of customer intent than any single source alone.

---

### Reporting Should Answer Business Questions

The project reinforced the importance of designing reporting around stakeholder questions rather than available metrics.

The most valuable insights emerged from connecting datasets and identifying patterns across customer behavior rather than creating additional dashboards.
