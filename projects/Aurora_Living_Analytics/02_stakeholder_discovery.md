# 02 — Stakeholder Discovery & Business Questions

## Purpose

In a real organization, the analyst should understand stakeholder priorities and business questions before finalizing measurement.

These are working assumptions for Aurora Living because it is a learning/test project.

## Executive / business owner

- What is the most important business outcome for the next 6–12 months?
- Is revenue growth the primary priority?
- Are acquisition, retention, awareness and email growth also priorities?
- What revenue target should be used?
- What does success after six months look like?
- Which customers, products or categories matter most?
- What decisions should analytics help leadership make?

## Marketing

- Which channels bring qualified visitors?
- Which channels generate purchases and revenue?
- Which channels grow newsletter subscriptions?
- Does email generate repeat purchases?
- Does email contribute to upsell revenue?
- Which campaigns contribute to discovery and sales?

## Merchandising / product

- Which products and categories receive the most attention?
- Which products are viewed frequently but purchased infrequently?
- Which product lists or recommendations drive product selection?
- Where do users abandon the product-to-cart journey?

## UX / website

- Can visitors easily discover products?
- Do product pages support purchase intent?
- Where do users drop between product view, cart, checkout and purchase?
- What friction affects checkout completion?
- Which interactions indicate meaningful engagement?

## Retention

- How many customers return to purchase again?
- What proportion of revenue comes from repeat customers?
- Does newsletter subscription correlate with later purchases?
- Which email interactions result in repeat purchases or upsells?

## Analytics

- Which KPIs should be reviewed regularly?
- Which events are necessary to answer the business questions?
- Which metrics are leading vs. lagging?
- What data-quality checks are required before reports are trusted?

## Technical

- Which interactions can GTM track with built-in functionality?
- Which require a custom listener?
- Which require developer-provided Data Layer data?
- What product and transaction data is required for GA4 ecommerce?
- How will implementation be tested before publishing?

## Validation rule

For a real client, these assumptions must be confirmed with stakeholders. We should not invent business goals on behalf of a decision-maker.
