# 03 — Measurement Plan

## Purpose

Translate business goals into website goals, KPIs, targets and measurable behaviors.

This is a **strategic measurement plan**, not the detailed GTM tag implementation plan.

## Goal 1 — Increase online revenue

### Website goals

- Increase completed purchases.
- Improve checkout progression.
- Reduce friction in the purchase journey.

### KPIs

- Ecommerce revenue
- Purchases
- Average order value
- Checkout progression
- Key-event rate

### Relevant GA4 ecommerce events

- `begin_checkout`
- `add_shipping_info`
- `add_payment_info`
- `purchase`

## Goal 2 — Sell more products

### Website goal

Improve product discovery and product engagement.

### KPIs

- Product-list views
- Product selections
- Product views
- Add-to-cart rate
- Purchases by product/category

### Relevant ecommerce events

- `view_item_list`
- `select_item`
- `view_item`
- `add_to_cart`
- `remove_from_cart`

## Goal 3 — Increase repeat customers

### Website goal

Build long-term customer relationships.

### KPIs

- Repeat-purchase rate
- Returning customers/users
- Revenue from repeat customers
- Purchase frequency where data supports it

## Goal 4 — Increase brand visibility / awareness

### Website goal

Improve qualified discovery and meaningful engagement.

### Potential KPIs

- Qualified traffic
- Engaged sessions
- Product discovery behavior
- Returning visitors
- Channel/campaign reach where appropriate

Awareness should not be reduced to a single vanity metric. The final KPI definition should be validated.

## Goal 5 — Grow newsletter subscriptions

### Website goal

Build the owned audience.

### KPIs

- Successful newsletter subscriptions
- Subscription rate
- Subscriptions by traffic source / landing page
- Later purchase behavior of subscribers, where appropriate and privacy-compliant

The actual event name and implementation should be selected after inspecting the real subscription form.

## Goal 6 — Increase email / upsell revenue

### Website goal

Encourage repeat purchases and upsell behavior.

### KPIs

- Email-attributed revenue
- Repeat-purchase revenue
- Upsell revenue
- Upsell conversion rate

## Target framework

- Working revenue target: **$200,000 after six months**
- Other KPI targets should be created after baseline data is available.
- Every target should have a number, timeframe and clear definition.

## Implementation priority

Start with the end of the funnel:

1. Purchase
2. Checkout
3. Cart
4. Product interactions
5. Discovery / engagement
6. Retention and newsletter behaviors

Do not attempt to implement everything at once.

## Status

**Complete — strategic measurement phase.**
