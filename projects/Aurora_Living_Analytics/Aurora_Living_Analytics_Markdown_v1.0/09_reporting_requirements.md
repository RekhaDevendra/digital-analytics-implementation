# 09 — Reporting Requirements

## Purpose

Define what stakeholders should be able to understand from analytics after implementation.

## Executive view

### Primary questions

- Are we growing online revenue?
- Are purchases increasing?
- Are repeat customers increasing?
- Are important website behaviors moving in the right direction?

### Core metrics

- Revenue
- Purchases
- Average order value
- Key-event rate
- Repeat-purchase indicators

## Product performance

Questions:

- Which products are most viewed?
- Which products generate the most purchases?
- Which products have strong views but weak add-to-cart performance?
- Which categories contribute most to revenue?

## Funnel performance

Suggested funnel:

**Product view → Add to cart → Begin checkout → Shipping → Payment → Purchase**

Analyze:

- Users/sessions at each stage
- Drop-off
- Progression rate
- Key-event rate

## Acquisition

Questions:

- Which channels bring qualified traffic?
- Which channels drive purchases?
- Which channels drive revenue?
- Which channels produce newsletter subscribers?

## Email / retention

Questions:

- How much revenue is associated with email traffic?
- Do subscribers return and purchase?
- What is the repeat-purchase contribution?
- Are upsells increasing?

## Reporting principle

A report should answer a decision-making question. Avoid dashboards that simply display every available GA4 metric.

## Future reporting layer

Once the tracking foundation is stable, build:

1. GA4 standard reports
2. Explorations for funnel/path questions
3. A stakeholder-facing dashboard where useful
4. Recurring performance review structure
