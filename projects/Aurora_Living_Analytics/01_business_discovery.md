# 01 — Business Discovery & Measurement Strategy

## Purpose

Define the business context before touching analytics implementation.

## Project context

Aurora Living is treated as a learning-project e-commerce website focused on home décor products. It is a test environment for analytics practice rather than a production client implementation.

## Products / offering

- Home décor products.
- Examples discussed: plants, desk-related items, reading/books and coffee-related products.
- The actual website catalog remains the source of truth for the final product description.

## Business goals — 6–12 months

1. Increase online revenue.
2. Sell more products and increase overall sales.
3. Increase repeat customers and repeat purchases.
4. Increase brand reach, visibility and awareness.
5. Grow newsletter subscriptions as an owned-audience channel.
6. Increase email-driven sales and improve upsell performance/conversion.

## Working revenue target

A target of **$200,000 in sales after six months** was discussed.

This is a working assumption for the learning project and should be validated with a real stakeholder in a production scenario.

## Website goals

- Help visitors discover products.
- Increase engagement with product content.
- Increase purchase intent, including add-to-cart behavior.
- Maximize completed purchases / checkout completion.
- Build long-term relationships through repeat purchases and newsletter subscriptions.

## Customer journey

**Discovery → Engagement → Purchase intent → Purchase → Relationship / retention**

The important lesson is that analytics should not jump directly from a business goal to a list of events. We first determine what the website needs to accomplish.

## Leading vs. lagging indicators

### Lagging

- Revenue
- Purchases
- Overall sales outcome

### Leading

- Product discovery
- Product views
- Add to cart
- Checkout progression
- Newsletter subscription
- Repeat activity

Leading indicators help explain why a lagging business outcome changed.

## Measurement philosophy

A measurement plan answers **what matters and why**. A technical tag implementation plan answers **how the data will be collected**.

The Analytics Mania course materials explicitly distinguish the measurement plan from the later tag implementation plan and recommend prioritizing important business interactions before expanding tracking.

- Simo Ahava resources for advanced GTM patterns where needed.
